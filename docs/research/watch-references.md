# Watch Reference Survey — what already exists, and where the gaps are

Goal: collect the ways real (and concept) watches already show time, break them down into
independent **difference axes**, and use the gaps to design concepts that are genuinely
different from the existing catalogue of the world.

> Survey compiled October 2026 from general horological knowledge plus targeted web searches
> (sources at the end). It is a design tool, not a claim of historical completeness — any
> originality claim still needs separate research (PROJECT_VISION.md §21).

## 1. Reference catalogue

| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

## 2. Difference axes

Every display above is a point in this space. A concept is "different" when it occupies a
cell nobody has filled, not when it changes the colour of an occupied one.

| Axis                          | Values already used                                                   | Under-used / open values                                                      |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **What moves**                | hands, discs, numerals, rollers, fluid, light, movement, balls        | the **case itself** relative to the strap; the reader's frame                 |
| **Encoding**                  | absolute angle, position on a line, digits, fill level, shadow, words | **relative angle between two parts**; **alignment of fragments**; size; count |
| **Where the reading happens** | everywhere on the dial, apertures                                     | at the **lug / strap** (outside the dial)                                     |
| **Legibility mechanism**      | always legible                                                        | **legible only where things line up** (noise elsewhere)                       |
| **One element, two values**   | rare (Ressence discs, wandering hours)                                | a single gesture carrying hour _and_ minute (direction + opening)             |
| **Motion character**          | sweep, jump, retrograde, flow                                         | **combination-lock** multi-ring jumps; a quarter-turn of the whole object     |
| **Coordinate system**         | polar around the centre, linear                                       | radial distance as a value; **rotated reference frame**                       |
| **Dimension**                 | flat dial, some 3D (spheres, cubes)                                   | the whole watch as a 3D moving sculpture                                      |
| **Mechanical character**      | gears, cams, fluid                                                    | scissors / pivot linkages                                                     |

## 5. Backlog (not yet built)

- **Count**: hour = number of lit petals / polygon vertices; minutes as rotation.
- **Size encoding**: the current hour numeral is the largest (a fisheye passing round the dial) — check against Relevo before building.
- **Tilting dish**: the dial plane tilts toward the hour; a marble rolls to the low point.
- **Strap display**: links of the bracelet flip to show the hour on the wrist's side.
- **Dual frame**: two time zones read from the same hands against two counter-rotating frames.
